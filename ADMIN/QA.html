CSV format

Required columns: section,topic,question,optionA,optionB,optionC,optionD,correctAnswer
Optional: explanation,order,active. correctAnswer accepts A/B/C/D or 1/2/3/4. The section and topic must already exist; names are matched case-insensitively. If a topic is missing, the row is skipped rather than creating unexpected structure.

Example: Agronomy,Cropping Systems,Which is a ...?,Option 1,Option 2,Option 3,Option 4,B,Explanation,1,true
